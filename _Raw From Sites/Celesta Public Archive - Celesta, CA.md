## Synopsis

The city of Celesta, CA is a major coastal city located on the Pacific coast of the [United States](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america?authuser=0), in the state of California. Founded in 1847 as Kyrieville, between two major rivers (Great California River and the Celesta River, known at the time as the River Kyrie) and in view of Mt. Kyrie (now Mt. Celesta), the town gained significance in 1849 as a major source of a previously undiscovered element in the Celesta River, [celestium](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/chemistry/celestium?authuser=0), named for its appearance and the shimmering glow it let off at night through the clear river waters. Over the following years, it would grow in population and gain a significant celestium mining industry, also taking a new name to reflect the new discovery. In the 1970s, it would find itself the heart of a new celesciences division at [CSLA](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/csla?authuser=0), which would cause the town to explode in size and importance over the coming decades. In the present day, it is a megalopolis with a land area greater than many major cities across the globe, being 30 miles wide in radius at its largest, and boasting a population well over 50 million.

Celesta has a surprisingly high Christian population for a city of its size. Despite this, it is still home to a large variety of beliefs and cultural identities. Being home to CSLA and its [UET Program](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/csla/uet-program-hq?authuser=0), Celesta has integrated cultures from across the ultraverse and its constituent multiverses, making it a culturally unique locale even by US major cities' standards. Additionally, Celesta boasts its own [regional Pokemon league](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/cpl?authuser=0), a [massive central library](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/cpml?authuser=0), and an extensive civil infrastructure.

Being the epicenter of the [first recorded](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/history/changewave-1?authuser=0) [Changewave](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/chemistry/celestium/changewave?authuser=0) phenomenon, Celesta is the subject of intense international study, and has found itself home to several military bases and federal oversight in an effort to prevent foreign sabotage. It also has one of the highest concentrations of [RVCS](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/medical/cvcs?authuser=0) in any regional population, the highest diversity in demographics, and the greatest number of new US innovators and entrepreneurs in the 21st century to date. This single city's existence has maintained and secured the US' status as a global superpower in the past 5 years as a result.

## Locales & Districts

### Central District

The densest, most well-developed district of Celesta proper, the Central District houses some of the tallest skyscrapers and largest civic buildings in the city, home to several corporations' headquarters and a thriving downtown. The majority of the city's traffic runs through this critical area.

Notable locations:

- Riley Clothing Conglomerate HQ
- Celesta City Hall
- Celesta International Airport (CXQA)
- [Celesta Public Metropolitan Library](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/cpml?authuser=0)
- [Peifer](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/characters/id-001-100/id-001-010/jeremy-david-peifer?authuser=0) - [Rachels](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/characters/id-001-100/id-001-010/jessica-marie-peifer?authuser=0) Penthouse Apartment
- Celesta International Harbor
- Celesta Central Urban Plaza
- Baked Bean Coffee Co. HQ & Cafes
- [Celesta Pokemon League](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/cpl?authuser=0) HQ & Main Arena
- Urban Medical Center
- Urban Central K-12

### Riverside District

One of the oldest districts in the city, the Riverside District houses many of Celesta's historic buildings and monuments, as well as many of its mid-rise brick apartments and mixed-use residential complexes. This is where many of the smaller offices and local businesses are housed as well.

Notable locations:

- Celesta Civic History Museum
- First Baptist Church of Celesta
- St. Mary's Cathedral
- Celesta Primary School
- Celesta Secondary School
- Old Civic Hall
- Pryor Historic Residence
- Kennedy Memorial Elementary School
- Celesta Police HQ
- Celesta Fire HQ

### Towers District

One of the more prominent tourist destinations of Celesta, the Towers District houses the earliest skyscrapers of Celesta and as such has a much lower skyline than the Central District. However, it is home to a thriving nightlife and is one of the busiest locales Celesta has after hours.

Notable locations:

- FBI Celesta
- Rhylian Embassy to the US
- Pacifican Embassy to the US
- Carnegie Tower
- Irish Heritage Hall
- Portuguese Heritage Hall
- Nightclub Serendipity

### Commons District

The Commons District is a medium-density district with a sharp contrast between low-rise skyscrapers and more suburban apartments built in the classic SoCal style. It also has a large subsection full of Bay Area-style row houses, making it a popular place to live.

Notable locations:

- Commons Plaza
- Celesta Central Bank HQ
- Celesta Common High School
- Celesta Common Elementary School
- St. Syris Rhylian Orthodox Cathedral
- Celesta DOE Offices

### Celesta Heights

Celesta Heights is one of the more central suburbs of Celesta, packed to the brim with brick apartments, modern apartments, and detached single-family homes surrounded by greenery, lush parks, and public utilities. The majority of characters live here.

Notable locations:

- Celesta Urban Indoor Pool
- Celesta Heights Elementary School
- St. Peter Catholic School
- Celesta Heights High School
- Celesta Museum of American History

### University District

The University District is home to CSLA and a few surrounding suburbs. It is built on the low-rising slope of Mt. Celesta.

Notable locations:

- [Celesta School of the Liberal Arts](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/csla?authuser=0)
	- [UET Program HQ](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/united-states-of-america/celesta-ca/csla/uet-program-hq?authuser=0)
- Celesta Core Elementary School
- Celesta Core High School
- Mt. Celesta
- Celesta River Hydro Power
- Celesta Central Celestium Mining Co. HQ

### Titans' Valley

The Titans' Valley district is named for the population demographic it holds and the sheer internal scale of the district. Home specifically to supertall individuals (in excess of 15' in height) and boasting a non-Euclidean geography (bigger on the inside than the outside), this district allows accommodations for individuals who would otherwise have to exercise shapeshifting in order to thrive in the city on a regular basis. It is also a popular tourist destination, boasting beautiful natural views and itself being a national park.

Notable locations:

- Titans' Valley National Park
- Mt. Prometheus

### Summervale

A rural and suburban region on the outskirts of Celesta, this region is considered one of the "border towns" which comprise the Greater Celesta Area. Summervale in particular is home to a great portion of Celesta's agriculture.

Notable locations:

- [Forrester Farms](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/characters/id-001-100/id-001-010/cylia-felicia-forrester?authuser=0)
- Summervale K-12
