## Celestium

[Synopsis](#h.ka41vkciumft)

## Synopsis

Celestium (Cx) is a mineral element that serves as a central concept in the ROTC universe. It does not have a standard mapping on the periodic table and therefore must be placed to one side. It has few standard properties, making it an extremely enigmatic and often unpredictable element for engineers and researchers to work with. However, common to celestium is a high concentration of reons (aka "reality particles"), subatomic particles capable of altering or manipulating reality, as well as being thrown as radiation. When celestium throws off reons, the resulting radiation is known as celestium radiation, celerad(iation), or C-RAD.

C-RAD is unlike typical radiation, in that it often has no harmful side effects upon irradiating a living being. However, this does not make it necessarily safe nor harmless. Due to the properties of reons, C-RAD can have extremely unpredictable results, and is a primary cause of genetic mutations that simply could not exist otherwise due to violations of standard physical law. For instance, [CVCS](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/medical/cvcs?authuser=0) was originally caused due to early researchers ingesting celestium (either airborne dust or actually eating samples) and becoming irradiated, reons altering the properties of the individual's DNA in ways that would only be discovered later. [Future excitement of celestium deposits](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/chemistry/celestium/changewave?authuser=0) in individuals' bodies and cells would cause more extreme cases of the condition. The same effects would be seen in cases of [cryolasia](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/medical/cryolasia?authuser=0).

Apart from this, celestium is noteworthy for its appearance (both as an ore and as a refined metal) and its limitless uses. As a raw ore, celestium generally takes on a black, magnetite-like appearance with veins and flecks of glowing blues and purples. When refined, the colors and glow intensify and become the main appearance of the metal, making it extremely valuable in jewelry design. The metal can be further refined and manipulated to develop a more dulled silver without a glow, and used as a more than viable replacement for materials like tungsten, steel, aluminum and titanium, or refined into a brass-like substance with superconductive properties. It also has been made into a silky fabric by Olivia Riley, who uses it as the core component of her smartwear technology, utilizing celestium's reality-manipulation properties to create clothing that adapts to the wearer's wants and needs. There are countless other resulting products, but these are the primary use cases one must know about.
