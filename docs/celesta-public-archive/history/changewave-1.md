# Timeline

## March 16, 2020

2:15 PM PST - [Jeremy Peifer](/celesta-public-archive/characters/001-100/001-010/oc-0001-jeremy-david-peifer.md) and [Jessica Rachels](/celesta-public-archive/characters/001-100/001-010/oc-0002-jessica-marie-peifer.md) get married.  
4:40 PM PST - The newly-wed couple adopts a Labrador - Golden Retriever - Cocker Spaniel mix puppy and names her Ettisynne.  
6:15 PM PST - With help from friends, the couple finishes moving into the penthouse suite gifted them by Jessica's childhood friend [Katrina](nocontent.md).

### March 17, 2020

1:15 AM PST - Residual, long-term excitement from getting married causes a buildup of hormones and neurochemicals which begin to interact with celestium in Jessica's cellular makeup as she sleeps. This begins a chain reaction.  
5:40 AM PST - The chain reaction begins going critical and the celestium in Jessica's cells enters reon-active state.

### \- MOMENT OF INCIDENCE -

5:45 AM PST - The celestium reaction goes supercritical and releases a reon burst with an energy equivalent of roughly 210-240 PJ (petajoules), about the same as the detonation of the Tsar Bomba. The celerad activity tears conventional reality apart, breaking down the boundaries between parallel universes and multiverses, sending reality into a shock state despite being invisible to the naked eye. The energy spike is picked up by satellites from several countries.

### \- AFTERSHOCKS -

6:00 AM PST - 911 dispatchers record incoming calls that make no sense; the calls describe impossible biological changes, unidentified strangers sleeping in their bed, wearing wedding rings they never had, etc.  
6:15 AM PST - The sheer influx of 911 calls disrupts emergency services and Celesta government officials are forced to put out an emergency alert advising people to remain calm and go about their business. If cause for alarm is found, they are given alternative hotlines to use to reduce the distress on emergency services. A formal investigation is launched into the cause of these occurrences.  
6:30 AM PST - Public panic sets in. People going to work suddenly begin shapeshifting unwillingly, gaining or losing height unexpectedly or outright shifting form and species. However, this is not yet widespread and is happening only occasionally, localized to a few specific parts of Celesta as celerad activity begins to spread slowly across the city, a side-effect of the rupture and reality shockwave screaming through the atmosphere.  
7:20 AM PST - The layout of Celesta begins to shift in real-time and undocumented individuals begin appearing across the city. Centaurs, [high elves](/celesta-public-archive/science/biology/elves/high-elves.md), [anthropomorphic creatures](/celesta-public-archive/science/biology/changed/index.md) and fictive creatures begin appearing as well. The Celesta mayor declares a state of emergency and calls on the California governor to request FEMA assistance.  
7:45 AM PST - Jeremy and Jessica wake to see Ettisynne sitting in her bed with an anthropomorphized form. Seeing little other choice, the two adopt her as their daughter and she takes a middle name, Erika, and the hyphenated surname Peifer-Rachels.  
8:25 AM PST - Hospitals across Celesta find their emergency rooms flooded with people panicking or unconscious due to shock of various causes. The first blood results come in revealing individuals with two sets of DNA and heightened celeradiation readings. Lacking any precedent or medical terminology for these results, the doctors coin the individual a "Changed."  
8:45 AM PST - Upon further readings, doctors and medical examiners find no indication of biohazard regarding the Changed, and advise the Celesta government of their findings. Official documentation states "there is no risk of harm, physical danger, or contagion from these individuals, so a biological or medical crisis can be ruled out. Whatever is happening is a fundamental rewrite of the laws of nature, and that is inherently a subject not to be addressed by medical personnel but by experts in their respective fields."  
9:15 AM PST - Reports of these changes become widespread across the city and begin spreading to towns on its outskirts. The California governor declares a state of emergency and begins demanding answers from the government of Celesta.  
9:30 AM PST - Investigations into the event's source pinpoint Jeremy and Jessica's home as the epicenter, but do not find any indication of foul play. With Jessica as their only lead, she is taken into custody pending further investigation and interrogation.  
10:30 AM PST - Review of Jessica's medical records reveal an unusually high concentration of celestium in her cellular composition. After consulting experts on the element, it is concluded that she is the original source of the event. Based on celerad readings, it is found that she could not have consciously caused this event, and she is transitioned to low-security holding pending federal examinations and investigation.  
10:40 AM PST - Reports come in from the San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, and Reno, Nevada, of similar occurrences to those in Celesta.  
11:15 AM PST - Astronomers detect massive EM interference in the atmosphere across the West Coast, and come back with startling findings. CMBR (Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation) readings are noisy and erratic compared to baseline, as if the 'fingerprint' of reality had become a conglomeration of a countless number of universes.  
12:10 PM PST - Seattle, Denver, and cities across northern Mexico begin reporting the same occurrences as in Celesta. With hospitals being flooded by the same kinds of test results as in Celesta, the event is dubbed the "Changewave" and is placed under intense international scrutiny. POTUS is forced to make a public statement on the matter.  
1:10 PM PST - Honolulu, Boston, Anchorage, and several Canadian cities report being affected by the Changewave. The San Francisco Bay Area is forced to triage on the streets due to overcrowding of emergency rooms across the area.  
1:55 PM PST - The situation in Celesta begins to calm as people realize they're in no immediate danger. People found to have changed forms are asked to come in for medical examinations and are marked as Changed. With a significant amount of medical histories in Celesta now showing Changed status, the term "Numan" begins seeing use.  
2:10 PM PST - The first reports of Changewave effects come out of South America and Greenland.  
3:20 PM PST - Japan, Russia, China, South Korea, the Philippines, Portugal and Spain all report that they are now being affected by the Changewave. US satellite data confirms the presence of a "reality shockwave" in the atmosphere that has reached 80% of the globe, with a dispersal pattern that matches that of the Tsar Bomba's shockwave.  
6:30 PM PST - Eastern Europe, Africa, India and the Middle East report being affected by the Changewave phenomenon, signifying the end of 'normalcy' as the world understood it in just over 12 hours.

### 2020

In the days and months following, the Changewave would pass over the Earth 3.5 times, warping the physical laws of reality into a new normal. Traditional physics would ultimately remain as expected, but scientists would gradually pick up on new and unexpected quirks. Doctors would begin recording more extreme cases of [CVCS](/celesta-public-archive/science/medical/cvcs.md) and [cryolasia](/celesta-public-archive/science/medical/cryolasia.md) as the new arrivals began filtering into medical facilities and being officially documented. Soon, documents and data that didn't previously exist would start stumping officials across the globe, as well as memories of friendships and relationships for people who found themselves the butt end of a "cosmic joke" where they got left out of a reality that had asserted itself in their lives.

Within the first week or so, Jessica would find herself undergoing medical examinations and federal debriefings, before being released back to her normal life, officials satisfied that there was no foul play involved.

In May 2020, [CSLA](/celesta-public-archive/locations/americas/north-america/usa/california/celesta/csla/index.md) would create a joint task force for exploring the alternate realities unlocked by the Changewave. This would become known as the [Ultraverse Expeditionary Team Program](/celesta-public-archive/locations/americas/north-america/usa/california/celesta/csla/uet-program-hq.md).

Celeradiation readings would gradually decay over the remainder of the year, but "aftershocks" in the form of smaller Changewave events would pop up from time to time. Localized reality shearing would also have the same effect, bringing in newcomers seemingly at random. By December 2020, the world population had grown beyond 30 billion and world order had shifted dramatically with the presence of nations that had not previously existed, the Changewave exposing a "hidden timeline" and merging it with the one the world had known before. In other words, the world itself changed, bringing with it a history and a geopolitical situation that hadn't existed prior to CW-1, prime examples being [Rhylia](/celesta-public-archive/locations/rhylian-continent/rhylia/index.md) and [Pacifica](/celesta-public-archive/locations/pacific-ocean/pacifica/index.md).
