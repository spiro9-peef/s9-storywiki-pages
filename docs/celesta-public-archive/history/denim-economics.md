## Synopsis

An economic event and ongoing phenomenon caused by the unexpected rise of the [Riley Clothing Conglomerate](/celesta-public-archive/science/sociology/economics/companies/riley-clothing-conglomerate.md), that is named for the widely scrutinized merger of the aforementioned company with the [Levi Strauss & Co.](/celesta-public-archive/science/sociology/economics/companies/levis.md) denim manufacturer in late 2022, which has maintained its name and legacy as a subsidiary of RCC, having merged with the company in a stroke of foresight in order to maintain relevance and additionally benefit from selling specialized, branded, pre-programmed smartwear that always maintains the Levi's branding and signature denim look and feel.
