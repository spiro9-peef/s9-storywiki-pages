# Republic of Pacifica
#### Nation | c. 1750 AD - present day

## Synopsis

The Republic of Pacifica is a primarily [merfolk](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/merfolk.md) and aquatic-species nation built on the Pacific seafloor, founded circa 1750 AD from scattered feudal states and tribes which lacked any form of real infrastructure or structured way of life, in response to the rapid development of the peoples of *taika pauga* (Pacifican for *"terra firma"*, the Pacifican terminology for the surface). In the 250+ years since its founding, Pacifica has gone from a third-world non-power to a seafloor superpower that has parity with [China](celesta-public-archive/locations/eurasia/asia/china/index.md), [Russia](celesta-public-archive/locations/eurasia/europe/russia/index.md), and the [US](celesta-public-archive/locations/americas/north-america/usa/index.md) in terms of raw military and technological capability.

Culturally speaking, Pacifica has a blend of ancient Greek, ancient Egyptian, Polynesian, omni-Japanese (that is, all eras of Japan), and modern American culture, primarily dominated by frescoes and Art Deco design. Pacifican architecture is a masterstroke of practical and creative engineering, blending retrofuture and steampunk design with Art Deco architecture and sci-fi pressure domes. As for language, the [Pacifican language](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/science/linguistics/pacifican?authuser=0) has striking similarities to modern Hawaiian, Japanese, and even hints of Spanish. However, in the second half of Pacifican history, English has started to become the dominant language due to American influence.

Scientifically, Pacifica lags behind their surface counterparts in the fields of aeronautics and astronomy, but excels in submersibles, atmospheric pressurization, robotics, sonar, nuclear fission, fusion and geothermal energy, and magnetic levitation technologies. As a result, Pacifica dominates the deep sea, and leads in energy and high-quality manufacturing. Pacifica also is a top exporter of rare earth elements found deep in the ocean, having developed relatively clean ways to extract minerals at rates that surface nations can only dream of. Due to these advantages, Pacifica has been considered a prime trading and research partner by the US for future joint space exploration missions in spite of its lacking space program.

## History

### Early History & Founding (0 - 1750 AD)

Prior to 1750, Pacifica was dominated by various different feudal and tribal societies primarily composed of specific species and cultures, the only real unifying factors being a hunter-gatherer focus, a proto-Pacifican language, and shared aquatic background. Some breakthroughs did occur over the years, and small settlements were founded across the seafloor, but none of these peoples or societies truly could claim that they were ever historic powers. However, as human civilization on the surface began to develop, these underwater peoples began to amass scraps and wreckage from battles, discarded garbage, and other flotsam reaching the sea floor. At first, these were mild curiosities, and no small source of amusement, but as civilization began to industrialize, and the waters began to stir, the founding peoples of Pacifica realized they needed to do something or risk being steamrolled by their peers above the waters.

Merfolk infiltrators were sent into the coastal societies around 1720 AD, tasked with gathering as much information as they could on the world above. Those who came back brought radical ideas and frightening news. These factors resulted in Pacifica's founding sometime around 1750 AD, with the creation of the city of Pacific City (or Kapakilanikoa in Pacifican, Kapakila being "Pacific," -ni turning "Pacific" into "Pacifica," and -koa meaning "City") and the first written Pacifican Constitution.

### Early Industrialization (1750 - 1845 AD)

Without a solid technological framework to build upon, like their surface counterparts, the early Pacificans of the 18th century had to get creative - and creative they did indeed get! Scientists observed undersea volcanoes and similar geothermal phenomena and realized they could use the natural heat to power their first industrial smelting facilities. In the process of designing the equipment, Pacifican engineers discovered the concept of a water wheel, as powered by steam and not by flowing water. By 1795, the Pacificans had the foundations of a manufacturing industry and early steam-powered motors - and their first submarines.

As technology from the surface continued to descend in the wake of battles or negligence, Pacifica began to develop their first deepsea rail infrastructure, deepsea telegraph lines, and steam-powered electrical infrastructure. Having also developed submarines, Pacifican engineers and botanists realized that they had the technology to create deep-sea pressurized structures - the perfect conditions for farming surface plants! By 1845, Pacifica had a society with rough technological parity to the United States, though a much more rugged frontier society that had few cities, barely any navy, and comparatively insignificant industrial capacity.

### Pacifican Renaissance (1845 - 1895 AD)

Sometime after 1845, Pacifica got their first taste of mainline surface-dweller steam engines and steel, as well as the first records of modern chemistry, genetics, biology and other sciences, and realized that even still, they were sorely lacking. Additionally, unrest was beginning to threaten to tear the still-young republic apart - reforms were desperately needed.

Pacifica began pouring its resources into patronage of the arts and sciences, to great effect. Study of surface cultures and mythology became the foundations of Pacifican unification, forming a basis upon which they looked to the future and saw Pacifica as the captor of human imagination. This dream would fuel Pacifican culture and science for 50 years - and the growth of Pacifica's major cities, including a major urbanization trend after 1870. By 1895, they had developed a society that would easily dwarf any surface society in undersea dominance, and had developed a technological and cultural hallmark reminiscent of what future generations would call "steampunk."

### Pacifican Dark Age (1895 - 1920 AD)

As the latter years of the Pacifican Renaissance faded into history, their culture began to stagnate, as disease and starvation took hold. The rapid urbanization and industrialization of the 19th century left major cities choked in deep-sea smog, and their pressure domes' farms simply could not keep up with demand. Additionally, the relative peace in the surface of the Pacific meant very little in the way of reverse-engineered technology was available. Cultural influences and technological breakthroughs would still come in, but as surface societies got better at navigating the waters and bringing resources between different places, the Pacificans found themselves starved and isolated. With no contact with the surface, an increasingly xenophobic culture, and a dying society, Pacifica declined slowly and painfully.

### The Deep Awakening (1920-1941 AD)

Pacifica found its miracle in the arrival of American culture and human citizenry. Though official contact was still quite a ways off, the introduction of Art Deco and mass media culture reinvigorated Pacifican society, and Pacifica began building new infrastructure to integrate surface-dwellers into their cities, and bring news and culture to every home in Pacifica at breakneck pace. Some aspects, such as the first flight of the Wright brothers, were dismissed as fantastical delusions of the surface, but the introduction of books such as [20,000 Leagues Under The Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty_Thousand_Leagues_Under_the_Seas) reinvigorated the "Pacifican Dream." Jazz, soul, and gospel music found their way to Pacifican society in the 1920s and 1930s, and radio took Pacifica by storm, followed closely by television.

But this new renaissance was never meant to last.

### The Deafening Horror (1941-1945 AD)

Pacifica suddenly found itself drowning in steel, wood, oil and bodies. World War II was happening above the waters and Pacificans found the waters shaken by deep rumbles and flashes of light they could not explain, dark oily clouds lit aflame sinking down and blocking out what little sun they got at the sea floor.

The sudden arrival of wartime technologies forced Pacifica to mobilize. Depth charges were disarmed and disassembled, and subsequently reverse-engineered. Oil and coal combustion were researched and developed. Steel factories, aluminum refineries, and other critical resource production facilities of the age popped up all over the sea floor, and Pacificans found themselves manufacturing submarines of all sorts, developing torpedoes, and even probing theoretical physics for the first time, all while being terrified out of their senses.

### The Pacific Power (1945 - 1990 AD)

Just as Pacifica found its new path and footing, the wreckage stopped coming. New technologies were no longer sinking to the bottom of the Pacific. The world above had grown silent, and the waters cleared once more. It took 3 months for Pacifica to realize what had happened.

However, Pacifica refused to fall back into another dark age. They'd seen what happened when they lost hope and lost initiative. They promised themselves to never again repeat that mistake. So, they began experimenting with the technologies they had, finding new ways to refine them, finding new mechanics and principles that the surface never thought of, using ideas only a deep-sea society could dream up.

Soon, their waters began to churn and hum with a noise they'd never seen before. The surface dwellers were getting closer to Pacifican cities, and centuries of secrecy were about to be undone. This was unacceptable - if they were discovered unprepared, they would be at the mercy of these violent peoples. The Pacifican government began to promote mass reproduction and higher education, and formed its first official espionage organization. By 1960, they were already infiltrating the major powers' governments and learning their secrets, taking what they saw as valuable information and applying it to their own needs.

The Cold War culture soon began to seep into the sea. Pacifica identified the United States and the [USSR](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/locations/eurasia/russia/ussr?authuser=0) as competitor states, and viewed the ideological conflict as the perfect opportunity to rise and dominate the waters. They copied off of the nuclear programs of both states, developing their first ICBM complexes and nuclear fission plants by 1971, and pursuing an early space program by 1982.

In 1989, the outside world got their first wake-up call to Pacifica's presence. A Pacifican sub encountered a US nuclear sub in Pacifican waters, and a standoff happened that nearly destroyed the world. Fortunately, no shots were fired, and the first envoys were exchanged as Pacifica finally opened its borders. The UN officially recognized Pacifica as a nation in 1990.

### A Superpower of the Sea (1990 - present)

Now exposed to the internet, international phone lines, and surface technologies that they hadn't seen yet, Pacifica began its second renaissance. Robotics and electronics research became a staple of Pacifican government contracts, and clean manufacturing swept the deep sea like a fever. Cities grew taller and larger, new domes being built for hydroponics, and the first mag-lev rails between major cities opening up whole worlds of trade. Industrial-scale mining operations secured the Pacifican economy, with massive deposits of rare earth minerals ensuring Pacifica could dominate world markets - or end them if things turned sour.

Pacifican culture swept the internet, intermingling with American mass media and Japanese media forms like anime. The three nations would soon develop an unexpected camaraderie, even in the wake of admission of long-term espionage.

By 2011, Pacifica had the first successful and commercially viable fusion reactor, and the groundwork for their first moon landing laid. They were now a superpower of the sea, a force to be reckoned with when it came to the Pacific - and a nation nobody dared cross.

## Demographics

The diversity of Pacifica is, similarly to that of the majority of surface locations, difficult to adequately categorize due to the sheer quantity of species. However, two categories can be noted immediately. Merfolk comprise 38.8% of Pacifica's population, being the dominant population and the original founders of Pacific City; humans likewise comprise 23.4% of the population, being ubiquitous and readily adaptable. The remaining population is a melting pot of other species, predominantly aquatic species but surface species are also present.

With a population of roughly 2.2 billion, Pacifica also has a remarkably dominant population for [post-Changewave](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/history/changewave-1?authuser=0) figures, compared to China's 4.6 billion, India's 4.4 billion, Russia's 1.8 billion, and the USA's 3.7 billion.

## Governance

The Pacifican government is a constitutional federal representative republic, headed by a five-member council of governors and a president, with an extensive congressional legislature and a strong judicial system.

The Congress of Pacifica is composed of three parts: the House of Representatives, the Senate, and the Commissariat of Territories. The Commissariat of Territories is a small council composed of elected officials representing each of Pacifica's territories and provinces, tasked to cooperate with the other two sections in developing federal law and budget in a way that pleases the populace.

The Pacifican military is almost exclusively a submarine navy, though in recent years they have developed their first aircraft carrier, equipped with 20 purchased F-15s and escorted by Pacifica's only five destroyers.
