# Kingdom of Rhylia
#### Nation | c. 1150 BC - present day

## Synopsis

The Kingdom of Rhylia is a primarily [Rhylian](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/aviabrids/avipines/rhylians.md), [Norlander](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/norlanders.md) and human nation on the [Rhylian continent](celesta-public-archive/locations/rhylian-continent/index.md), founded circa 1150 BC as a series of feudal states meant to organize the small farming and fishing villages along the northern coastline. It would become an early empire (c. 900 BC) and develop the first air force (c. 350 BC). In present day, it is a regional power with strong ties to the [United States](celesta-public-archive/locations/americas/north-america/usa/index.md) and a firm alliance with its neighbor [Aleria](celesta-public-archive/locations/rhylian-continent/aleria/index.md).

Culturally speaking, Rhylia is a mix of Greco-Roman, European, American, and high-fantasy cultural influences. Many of its older cities and its architectural styles reflect its unique history, blending the old with the new. Rhylia also has a rich linguistic history (Old, Middle, and Modern Rhylian, Norkriki (Old and Modern Norlander), Rhylic Latin, Roman Imperial Latin, Koine Greek, and English) and a long-standing pervasive Christian tradition, with ornate cathedrals and chapels dotted all around the nation.

Scientifically, Rhylia is considered a side-grade to the US, leaning more heavily into their unique context, the high [dragon](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/dragons/index.md) population, and the presence of [celestium](celesta-public-archive/science/chemistry/celestium/index.md) deposits (and the resulting capabilities of Rhylian citizens to utilize celepsionic powers) in order to develop a 21st century economy and military with a strong fantasy lean. They are able to hold their own as a regional power but do not see any need to present themselves as a superpower. However, in recent years, they have started an effort to rapidly industrialize their nation, in order to ensure that they do not require outside assistance in case of a major conflict.

## History

### Lordship Era (1150 - 900 BC)

Early in Rhylian history, the countryside of its northern shores was sparse, the people having just barely developed bronze and iron working and still heavily relying upon local agrarian and fishing efforts to sustain small populations. There were few towns, and large, migratory and nomadic groups eking out a modest living were commonplace on the fields and in the forests. Some of the larger towns' leaders realized they could use the extra hands and workforce to strengthen their economy and build a better society, so they appointed feudal lords to govern regions as local leaders brought these nomadic clans into the fold.

At this time, the human population of the continent was effectively nil. Most people were either what would become the modern High Rhylian, or some other kind of anthropomorph, the highest percentage being of various owl species, and the Norlanders had not yet been discovered, their homes being far too distant for feudal Rhylia to encounter.

Over the next 250 years, the various feudal lords would expand their territories, build new roads and infrastructure, and establish trade... or conquer their neighbors, in some cases. The population grew, and new towns and villages were built. However, in one lordship, something new was brewing...

### Nascent Rhylia (900 - 718 BC)

One of the lordships, at home in a city called [Rhel](celesta-public-archive/locations/rhylian-continent/rhylia/rhylian-heartland/city-rhyle/index.md), found itself facing a unique situation. The population was booming, and there was plenty of food. So much, in fact, that much of it was rotting in storehouses and affecting good, fresh harvests negatively. The ruling lord, a High Rhylian by the name of Kors Nilani, realized that if he did not act fast, he would have a crisis on his hands. He sent out messengers to nearby lordships and offered trade agreements. When these lordships saw the sheer quantity of food that was being offered, at such low rates, they accepted. It was not long before the Rhel lordship found itself the de facto ruling party of the region. The feudal states reorganized themselves into a proper monarchy, each feudal lord accepting roles as regional governors under the watch of a central Nilani ruler.

By the time of the death of Kors and the coronation of his daughter Syr around 50 years later (c. 850 BC), the new Kingdom of Rhel was thriving and expanding its borders. Proper infrastructure, a shared currency, intercity trade and barter, and a shared language (Old Rhylian) meant unprecedented regional growth and wealth, bringing along with it new innovations and a thriving culture. Census programs were instituted and an advisory council was implemented to advise Syr as she continued her father's legacy.

Under Syr's rule, first contact with Norlanders was established. The Norlanders already had a unified culture and a form of government similar to the classical Athenian democracy, and called themselves the Nokriko, but brought with them an animistic polytheism which clashed with the Rhelic monotheistic belief system (similar to ancient Judaism, but little else is known). Though uneasy, trade was agreed upon and goods were bartered, bringing new resources, animals and crops to the lands of Rhel.

Syr's successor, Kais, took the throne in 785 BC, and nullified these trade agreements. The Nokriko civilization, having come to rely on the food from Rhel's more fertile farmland, entered a crisis, and took to war to attempt to resolve it. The resulting conflict lasted 35 years and ended with the capitulation of the Norkriko, whose population was a fraction of what it had once been. Kais was assassinated in 748 BC, using poison - killed by one of his own generals, who was angry about the war.

His successor, Isata, took the throne and continued the legacy of her grandmother Syr, establishing a relief effort to the Norkriko. She also began the first expeditions into the northeast meadows and the southern savannahs, looking to expand Rhel's territory. However, one of these expeditions returned with a plague in tow, killing 30% of Rhel's 1.5 million people, ultimately including Isata herself in 718 BC.

## First Nylani Dynasty - Kingdom of Rhylia
#### 900 BC - 2006 AD
---

### Prophetic Era (718 - 560 BC)

Isata's heir, Kioni, took the throne in 718 BC. However, unlike any of her predecessors, Kioni was afflicted by visions and dreams that destabilized her rule. She saw great winged, scaly beasts that breathed fire and ice, and chariots of metal flying through the sky. She saw the palace in Rhel, drowning in flames made of darkness, lit up by a girl with Kioni's face, but neither her voice nor clothes. These visions disturbed her deeply, traumatizing her and rendering her deeply paranoid. She started a civil war from increasingly erratic laws in 701 BC before dying months later in 700 BC.

Kioni's successor, Kolys, took the throne in 700 BC, and changed his name to Karis Vir Nylani, which at the time meant "glorious harbinger of the sword." He ended the civil war with the capture of Alea and its governor, then abdicated the throne to his eldest daughter, Syr II, in 697 BC.

Syr II's first act after being coronated was to rename the kingdom from Rhel to Rhaelea, and issued a formal apology to the regions of the Alean rebellion along with a promise to repeal the laws her ancestor Kioni implemented. She abdicated the throne in 682 BC after her father was poisoned by the son of the Alean governor, although he ultimately survived the attempt.

Her successor, Maris, took the throne, and only ruled for 8 days, falling into a coma after having similar visions to those of Kioni.

Maris' son, Karis II, would take the throne. He expanded Rhaelea's borders into the region now known as Pheasant Point, stumbling upon disparate tribes of [slimes](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/slimes/index.md) who would attack on sight. In 680 BC, repeated and increasingly organized attacks on Rhaelean scouts led Karis to declare war, resulting in the near-complete genocide of said tribes. Those who remained, sued for peace in 664 BC, on the condition that the region not be heavily settled, as it was considered a holy site to their people. Karis begrudgingly agreed, and the remaining tribes were assimilated into Rhaelea, though their numbers were low. Reports would reach the palace over the following years of hostile slime sightings and encounters in the region, dismissed as peasant rumors and fearful hearsay.

Maris awoke from her coma in 661 BC and wrote down what she saw in her visions. Karis II would abdicate and return the throne to Maris, who would continue to rule until 658 BC, when she died and left Karis II to resume rule. He died in 652 BC.

Karis II's son, Kilrys, would take the throne. New reports of hostile slime sightings would reach him, and unlike his predecessors, he would view them with concern. With permission from the slime population in Rhaelea's borders, he went out to Pheasant Point with a military detachment. He was met with [feral slimes](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/slimes/droppiis.md). He fought off a small pack of these, and then returned home with the news. Shortly thereafter, he fell ill, rambling about visions of the region covered in dead slimes as a darkness loomed overhead. He died in 648 BC, the slimes subsequently starting a rebellion, believing the Nylani line to have been cursed.

Kilrys' daughter Aldriel would take the throne. However, unlike her father, she was averse to battle and refused to send military forces to deal with the rebellion. Instead, she offered the slimes amnesty and passage back to Pheasant Point. This offer was accepted, but resulted in mass riots across Rhaelean core territories that led to Aldriel abdicating. She would take her own life shortly thereafter.

Her son, Mathiel, would take the throne in 647 BC. Facing the threat of a new civil war across the land, and threats by core regions' governors against the slimes in the Pheasant Point region, he was forced to figure out a solution and fast. Knowing the public concern that the Nylani line was permanently tainted or cursed, he sent out a message to the governors of Rhaelean territories, asking them to come with him to Pheasant Point so he could make a public showing for forgiveness by the slimes, for his ancestor disturbing their home. Skeptical, but also fearing needless bloodshed, they agreed. The slimes ran at the approach of the caravan, leaving behind only three tribal priestesses who insisted on staying in their homeland. After days of talk, these three ultimately agreed to put Mathiel through a religious ceremony to absolve him of his ancestors' guilt. The remainder of Mathiel's reign was peaceful, and he died in 620 BC.

His daughter, Lothia, would take the throne. She left her advisors in charge while she went alone to Pheasant Point, having had a vision of a great light in the region. She was at first captured by the slimes there, but was freed when she began having visions of light coming from the region and fighting a great darkness hovering above the streets of Rhel. The slimes agreed to reintegrate themselves into Rhaelean society after she experienced these visions, building small villages that would cement Rhaelean presence in the region permanently. She returned to the palace, but was forced to abdicate in 605 BC when she took ill, the visions growing more intense.

Lothia's son, Mathiel II, would take the throne. Mere days after his coronation, reports from the Norkriko territories in the northwest came in, of something they called Dikiako (dragons). Apparently, they were becoming more active and threatening villages - and if this pattern continued, they'd reach the Rhaelean heartland within a decade, threatening Rhel itself. Convinced that he needed to do something, he began preparing for war. He would bring Rhaelean soldiers to the Norkriko villages - many would fall in battle. Unfortunately, this included Mathiel himself, and he died in a blaze fighting a dragon in 599 BC.

His daughter's coronation would be interrupted by a delirious Lothia, who barged into the throne room raving about a battle for Rhaelea's very soul, before collapsing dead. The coronation continued, but the new queen Sathal was shaken. Knowing how her father died, she became convinced that the battle was against these dragons. She left her advisors in charge as she went alone to fight the dragons. She returned months later, with battle scars and a burn on her neck, with a sword in hand and a dragon in tow, speaking in verse, about a promise and a reclaiming queen, who would cleanse the land backed by a dragon, to unseat a wretched man from the throne. Believing her to be speaking about her victory in defeating the dragons that had killed her father, new songs were written about her. However, over the course of her rule, she began rambling about these messages again, with increasing fervor, intensity, and frequency. She would be forcibly removed from power in 570 BC, but she would continue to record her experiences and visions until her death in 560 BC. Her final writings would be kept in the palace archives, revered as poetry and prophecy, as her successor's future actions were eerily well-described in them.

### Heroic Era (560 - 236 BC)

Sathal's son, Mathiel III, would take the throne in 570 BC. Awestruck by the imagery (statues, frescoes, etc.) of his mother returning with a subdued dragon in chains and a sword in hand, he ruled with a "mandate of heroes," calling the Rhaelean people to tame the dragons and become their betters, as a trial of heroics. He also built up the palace using Norkriko marble and gold from the slimes' northeastern territories, including Pheasant Point, building the foundations of the modern Rhylian palace. By the end of his reign in 523 BC, the Rhaelean and Norkriko peoples were already testing the waters with dragon and wyvern riding.

His successor, Kioni II, would take the throne and begin commanding massive works projects in the northeast and the south, introducing massive reservoirs, canals and irrigation to sun-parched regions, and enabling the northeast to begin developing itself as a true part of Rhaelea, same as the Norkriko had done centuries before. Under her rule, the neighboring country of Aleria was discovered in 501 BC, and oceanic trade was established between the two. Aleria's human and [elvish](celesta-public-archive/science/biology/elves/high-elves.md) population began to migrate to Rhaelean society in the following decades, turning them from extreme minorities into a noteworthy percentage of the population. Likewise, Norkriko and slime populations would begin to migrate to Aleria. Kioni II would abdicate the throne in 470 BC, dying in 460 BC.

Kioni's successor, Sathal II, would take the throne and begin ordering land surveys to map out the surrounding regions. The first use of dragon riders for government purposes was found here, in exploring the southern regions and the surrounding [Pacific Ocean](celesta-public-archive/locations/pacific-ocean/index.md) to get accurate maps drawn up, though their range was limited. Sathal, realizing this was limiting Rhaelea's chances to explore, began to encourage colonization to the south, so that at least the landmass there could be mapped, and dragons with their riders might find places of rest in the process. She would die in 402 BC, leaving behind a postal service (434 BC) as one of her longest-lasting legacies.

Sathal's successor, Lothia II, would take the throne, and begin tasking her subjects with finding ways to keep dragons and their riders in the air for longer. In the process, many soldiers would begin experimenting with dragon mounts, finding new techniques for battle and new challenges to face in the absence of war. The postal service her predecessor left behind would be expanded to include dragon riders, enabling mail to find its way across Rhaelea faster than ever before, resulting in an innovation boom that would plunge Rhaelea into an early renaissance, just as Lothia II reached the end of her life in 352 BC.

Lothia II's successor, Kors II, would take the throne, being met with petitions from military leaders to organize the dragon-mount soldiers into an elite force. He would reject the proposal each time, citing the lack of active conflict, but in 350 BC he finally capitulated, forming the Rhaelean Draconic Corps., the first air force on Earth. Knowing that this would not be enough, from the sheer persistence of his generals, he began tasking the RDC with scouting and survey missions, being surprised at the efficiency compared to the efforts of his predecessors. Realizing he'd hit on a source of national pride, he began expanding the RDC, and Rhaelean culture began to reflect the newfound pride in its dragon population. Seeing this, he began the construction of a dragon arena in Rhel, which would be completed in 314 BC. He would be a regular attender of the tournaments held there, until suffering from a heart attack in the stands in 288 BC.

His successor, Othrys, would take the throne, just as Rhaelea's efforts to explore began producing surprising fruit. New lands had been discovered to the northeast and southwest (what we know today as the Americas and Australia). Seeing the reports with wide eyes, he immediately expanded the RDC and ordered them to begin mapping these new regions with urgency. By the end of his reign in 236 BC, he had established Rhaelea's first contact with Southeast Asia and the [Au Lac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%82u_L%E1%BA%A1c) people.

### The Great Humbling (236 BC - 110 AD)

Othrys' successor, Kais II, took the throne in 236 BC. What he had to work with, was the end of an era. Rhaelea was no longer alone, and it now had to prove its worth in a world far larger than it ever could have imagined. The heroics that drove them to this point were no longer enough to sustain their momentum - they had to be more. They had to be legends. Kais decided that this was quite simply impossible - it was not the Rhaeleans who had enabled themselves to reach this point, it was the presence of the dragons they rode and cherished. Nonetheless, he knew the Rhaelean people would not accept this. So, he pushed the RDC further, funding it and expanding it more than any of his predecessors, meeting the ancient [Chinese](celesta-public-archive/locations/eurasia/asia/china/index.md), [Japanese](celesta-public-archive/locations/eurasia/asia/japan/index.md) and [Korean](celesta-public-archive/locations/eurasia/asia/korea/index.md) peoples, the predecessors of Native Americans, and reaching Rhaelea's knowledge of the world deeper into Eurasia and into the Atlantic (from the Americas). New ideas came flooding into Rhaelea, and Rhaelea's culture and language began to change. Kais died in 188 BC, leaving his successor a changing kingdom.

That successor, Therys, realized that these new ideas were stirring new desires in the populace. He decided to allow his subjects to attempt limited trade with Asia, reaching out to Aleria to get their help in shipbuilding. Additionally, he further expanded the RDC, pushing it to explore further and further. He died just before the first scouts would encounter the steppe peoples of Europe, in 121 BC.

His successor, Lothia III, would take the throne and be the first to encounter the "barbarians" of Europe through messages returned from the RDC. The maps at her disposal were staggering, unlike anything she could put into words. Rhylia (as it was now known due to the changes in language) was a small part of a huge world, and her scouts were thousands of miles away from Rhyle. Her infatuation with this knowledge led her to push the RDC to explore as far as possible, and to begin encouraging the development of wyvern and dragon trade crews, using multiple in formation to carry large amounts of goods across long distances. Her efforts would be successful a mere three years before her death in 82 BC, though these crews could only operate locally between Rhylia and Aleria.

Lothia's successor, Kors III, would take the throne just as official contact with Rome was established. Enamored with the clean lettering of their script and the reports of both Greek and Latin languages, and the Greco-Roman culture, Kors ordered the RDC to focus its efforts on establishing official relations with the empire across the map. At first, it was just a trickle of exchanged information, but over the course of his reign, Rome and Rhylia began to grow closer as peers, exchanging information the other never knew could exist. Kors died at the peak of this exchange in 2 BC, dying before one of the most important series of historical events would occur.

His successor, Kais III, took the throne, returning the RDC's primary role to exploration, but still maintaining relations with Rome. Within three years of his rule, the RDC had been the first to circumnavigate the globe, and a direct path from Rhyle to Rome above the Americas and across the Atlantic was established. He also began pursuing his grandmother's efforts to carry out aerial trade over longer distances, finding some success restricted to the distance between Rhylia and Australia. He died in 27 AD from a stroke.

His successor, Karis III, took the throne just as scouts were returning reports of a major stirring in the region of Judea, of spiritual and political significance. Unlike his predecessor Kais, who would have dismissed this report, Karis was intrigued by the spiritual history of his own subjects, and saw this new information as worth investigating. He ordered the RDC to keep bringing new information about this development. When he heard the Roman response to Christians, the crucifixion of Christ, and how the young, nascent church was being persecuted, he began to cut Rhylia's ties with Rome, moving simultaneously to learn as much about this new faith as he could. He died before he could receive the information, in 72 AD.

His successor, Mathiel IV, would be the one to receive these reports. Fragments of early church letters, old Hebrew scriptures, and reports of the Roman abuses against the early church all reached him at once. He decided that Karis was unwise to cut ties with Rome, instead viewing his duty as a fellow ruler to be one of advisement to the Roman emperor. So, he began issuing letters to Rome advising them to take a more lenient and forgiving stance towards the church, all while learning more about the young Christian faith. When Mathiel died in 110 AD, he did so as the first Christian ruler of Rhylia.

With his death, a line from the prophecy returned to Rhylian memory. "A bright light returned on wings that shadow the land forever."

### Reclusion Era (110 - 600 AD)

Following Mathiel IV's death, his successor Cyana took the throne. She realized that the Roman Emperor was not going to listen to Rhylia's petitions, as trade had started to decay and some scouts were disappearing. She decided it was time to cut ties with Rome, but to honor her father she kept RDC spies within their territory. She knew, however, that even this would not be able to last, and as she began to explore the faith her father had died believing in, she realized that Rome was increasingly morally bankrupt and doomed ultimately to collapse. She had no time to waste in pulling Rhylia's men and resources out of Rome, especially if she wanted to leave a legacy devoid of that moral bankruptcy. She implemented a policy for the Nylani family - all their children would be educated in basic ethics, their civic duties, and the reasons why those things were important, beyond a theological perspective and into a practical one. She died in 168 AD, having brought the Christian faith into Rhylia for good, and having established a permanent legacy for the Nylani bloodline that would leave future Nylani rulers obligated to benevolence.

Her successor, Kais IV, took the throne, and began construction of the first cathedral in Rhyle. Additionally, he continued his mother's work of pulling men out of Rome, even bringing home a few Christians from overseas (inadvertently introducing some heretical doctrines in the process). He decreed Christianity the state religion by the end of his reign in 220 AD.

His successor, Mathiel V, took the throne and found Rhylia at risk of civil war. The Norkriko people and slime traditionalists were upset at the imposition of Christianity, and viewed the change in leaders as a prime opportunity to do something about it. Realizing the mistake of trying to force conversion, he lightened the decree of Kais, keeping Christianity as the state religion, but no longer mandating conversion, instead opting to promote it. This was not enough to stop riots from breaking out in major cities, but it did prevent a civil war from breaking out. In fact, his handling of the situation, especially once the riots started, caused more conversions to Christianity than had ever happened before in Rhylian history, many viewing the shift in policy as a rational victory for Christian ethics. He was unfortunately assassinated in 234 AD by one of the original agitators.

His successor, Cyana II, took the throne and implemented a crackdown (note: not an inquisition, just a crackdown) resulting in the arrest and execution of the original agitators. She also ordered heightened military presence in the fringes of Rhylian territory, and implemented the use of proto-Middle Rhylian terminology for the slimes and Norkriko people, naming them what would today be called "Gels" and "Northlanders" respectively. However, her brother Altion saw these acts as a desecration of Cyana I's legacy, and challenged Cyana II in the public sphere. When she attempted to have him arrested and executed, she was arrested and executed by one of her top military leaders, and Altion crowned king in her place in 237 AD. He loosened some of her policies and kept others, but also implemented subsidies for Norlander territories and the Northeast Region (including Pheasant Point), which garnered the favor of these peoples. By the end of his rule in 268 AD, Rhylia was fully culturally united. He died in 270 AD.

His successor, Cyana III, took the throne and began the thorough settlement of the Northeast Region's coastlines, aiming to give Rhylia a naval edge. She also continued the work of pulling Rhylia out of foreign affairs, resulting in the return of entire divisions of the RDC over the course of her reign. She died in 315 AD.

Her successor, Altion II, took the throne and began ending trade deals with the outside world. As the last vestiges of the RDC returned home in 327 AD, they brought with them detailed records of events such as the [Council of Nicaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Council_of_Nicaea), and early canons of the Bible. As a consequence, his rule presided over the official founding of the Rhylian Orthodox Church. He also reinforced his ancestor, Cyana I's legacy of benevolent rule, condemning the actions of Cyana II and praising the actions of Altion I, mandating that this position be taught to future generations so that the people might hold his successors to higher standards. He died in 385 AD, having completed the tasks Cyana I started.

His successor, Altion III, was left with a very isolated Rhylia, only trading actively with Aleria and sending out the occasional RDC scouting party to keep up on world events without trying to overextend. His policies were consequently focused on building up Rhylian society. He died in 427 AD.

His successor, Kais V, took the throne and continued his father's work until his death in 488 AD.

His successor, Mathiel VI, took the throne and continued his father's work until his death in 540 AD.

His successor, Cyana IV, took the throne and continued her father's work until her death in 600 AD.

### The Quiet Era (600 - 1325 AD)

Cyana IV's successor, Kato, took the throne in 600 AD. By this point, Rhylia had effectively stopped sending out scouts. The homefront was peaceful and thriving. Relations with Aleria were great. Kato realized Rhylia had something most nations could only dream of: true internal peace. And to him, that was the ultimate realization of Cyana I's legacy. He ordered the downsizing of the RDC and ushered in a new agrarian and frontiersman era. Temples were built across the land, reminders of the ancient prophecy of his forebears, and unbeknownst to him, they would become remarkably important places in future centuries. He died in 650 AD.

The history of Rhylia is not very noteworthy for several centuries, so a list will be given of his successors.

1. Cyana V (650 - 695 AD)
2. Kato II (695 - 730 AD)
3. Kais VI (730 - 788 AD)
4. Mathiel VII (788 - 820 AD)
5. Altion IV (820 - 855 AD)
6. Karis IV (855 - 891 AD)
7. Lothia IV (891 - 920 AD)
8. Cyana VI (920 - 980 AD)
9. Cyana VII (980 - 1000 AD)
10. Altion V (1000 - 1070 AD)
11. Altion VI (1070 - 1120 AD)
12. Mathiel VIII (1120 - 1183 AD)
13. Karis V (1183 - 1210 AD)

Karis V's successor, Lothia V, took the throne in 1210 AD, and found an alarming new trend: the dragons were disappearing. They were found retreating to Norlander territory, deep in the northern coastal mountains, or across distant shores to lands unknown or forgotten. And it was happening increasingly often. There are surviving letters from her to family and Rhylian governors, expressing concern that this could be a bad omen for Rhylia's future, or a divine punishment for failure to use such a critical resource. She died in 1280 AD.

Her successor, Lohar, continued to witness this trend. Then, in 1325 AD, months before he died, the presence of dragons seemed to come to a complete halt. He died afraid, not knowing what had come of a once-great land and Rhylia's pride.

### Forging A New Future (1325 - 1550 AD)

Lohar's successor, Kato III, took the throne in 1325 AD. Seeing the sorry state Rhylia was in, and lacking the national pride it once had in its dragon populace, he realized he needed to do something to keep his ancestors' legacy - and his country's legacy - alive. His answer was in technological and industrial development. Development of modern steel, alchemical research, and similar avenues of exploration became the new focus of the nation. The first smokestacks began being erected in Rhylia over the coming years, and engineers across the country began figuring out how to design sailing ships far superior to anything they'd made before. Rhylia, though completely isolated from the outside world, was still managing to keep pace with it, in its own way. Kato III died in 1390 AD, this being his legacy.

His successor, Mathiel IX, took the throne and continued Kato's legacy. The first caravels were built in 1419 AD, and began exploring the oceans once more. Their presence in the seas brought in merfolk and aquatic populations from the Pacific, who were intrigued by the presence of ships so far out. This new maritime tradition became a source of renewed pride for the Rhylian people. Mathiel died in 1440 AD.

His successor, Cyana VIII, continued the new naval tradition, and sent ships towards the Americas, hoping to renew contact with their peoples and engage in Rhylia's first cross-continental maritime trade. She would succeed with the Mayans and Incas, bringing back stories of new, but woefully primitive empires across the sea, reminiscent of Rhylia's ancient history. She would die in 1490 AD.

Her successor, Mathiel X, took the throne and continued sending caravels out to the Americas. When he received reports of other ships in the Americas in early 1493, he was ecstatic. This was the first time Rhylia had encountered a near-peer civilization in centuries, and he was the one to be there when it happened. Unfortunately, the Europeans (under Columbus) had brought plague and disease with them, and Rhylia suffered a devastating 40% loss in its 21 million population. This plague ultimately took Mathiel's life in 1495.

His successor, Kato IV, continued the work of establishing contact with foreign powers from across the sea. He tried to prevent the attacks on Mesoamerican nations, but was unsuccessful. Pride wounded, he recalled some of the ships from the Americas and sent them in the direction of Australia. He took his own life in 1550 after hearing news that one of the last three remaining ship crews in the Americas was slaughtered by unfamiliar weapons that billowed smoke and fire, realizing that these new powers were more capable than Rhylia ever had been. His country was obsolete, and in his eyes, so was he.

### The Age of Blood (1550 - 1765 AD)

Kato IV's successor, Cyana IX, took the throne in 1550 AD. Realizing these new nations were a far greater threat to Rhylia than anything the nation had faced before, she began sending Rhylia's fleets to Asia, hoping to make a dash for technologies and goods that might give them an edge. What they found shocked them - influence from those same nations, spreading across the continent. Cyana was troubled by this, and realized she was going to have to start making deals with these countries if she ever hoped to keep Rhylia even the slightest bit relevant. After gathering information, she made a difficult decision and began forming trade agreements with the British and Dutch. This brought the new technologies and goods that Cyana was looking for, but it also brought the Anglican Church, Protestantism, and the Roman Catholic Church, which had been hitherto unheard of on Rhylian shores. By 1585, Rhylia was embroiled in an internal spiritual war as three sects of Christianity began conflicting with one another: the Rhylian Orthodox Church, the Anglican Church, and the Roman Catholic Church. When the English and Spanish began fighting later that year, Rhylia was inadvertently dragged into a formal conflict with the Spanish, who had heard reports of the spiritual situation in Rhylia and believed them to be heretical. Cyana had gotten her wish to remain relevant - but not in the way she had hoped. She died in 1588.

Her successor, Kristiana Elizabeth Nylani, took the throne in 1588. She petitioned the English and Dutch for schematics on shipbuilding, cannon production, and the manufacture of gunpowder. Citing her own secretive Protestant faith in these letters, she asked royal-to-royal to be given the opportunity to raise a Protestant kingdom that could maintain its sovereignty and hold the Spanish at bay, in exchange for opening the borders further to their countries. It took over three years, but in June of 1592, her petition was answered. She immediately tasked Rhylia's navy with building new drydocks and building Rhylia's first ships of the line, and even began encouraging coastal raids on Spanish colonies in the Pacific by Rhylia's first privateers. When the first battles between Rhylian fleets and Spanish trade ships happened, they were laughably one-sided. Rhylia had sent a message to the Spaniards, that they would not be trampled upon. Unfortunately, Kristiana was assassinated in 1598 by Rhylian Orthodox and Roman Catholic inquisitors.

Her successor, Kristiana II, took the throne and found Rhylia suddenly embroiled in a civil war. Siding with the Protestants, she publicly decried her mother's death as an unjustified persecution of a royal who only cared for Rhylia's future. The military was ordered to seize ROC cathedrals and chapels within the heartland, and the navy was partially recalled to patrol Rhylian waters, but she refused to order any direct assaults on the Orthodox and Catholic forces. This order worked in her favor - she was preserving Rhylian history, and when the Catholic forces refused to stand down, it compounded upon her mother's death and created a series of martyrs in the Protestants' eyes. The resulting response by the populace was total and devastating, as counter-inquisitions were launched. In 1613, Kristiana II died leading a military effort to stop the fighting by force - killed ironically by a Protestant bullet.

Her successor, Kato V, ordered a complete crackdown on the fighting. Any agitators were to be arrested and executed, regardless of theological stance - no infighting among brethren would be tolerated. He ultimately decided to secularize Rhylia after two years of strict crackdowns, enabling the populace to believe as they chose. He abdicated in 1616, citing his actions as unbecoming of the Nylani bloodline.

His successor, Kristiana III, took the throne in 1616, and faced the first invasion of Rhylia by the Spanish, who were acting not only as a military force, but as an inquisition. Landing in the Norlander territories, the Spanish began burning villages and seizing others to plant their flag there. Protestants were publicly executed en masse. Kristiana ordered the total mobilization of the populace, and found even the Catholic and Orthodox populations surprisingly willing to defend their land against their former supporters. The Rhylian fleets were completely recalled to blockade the Spanish occupation while Rhylian troops moved in great numbers (nearly 1 million conscripts, volunteers, and enlisted soldiers). Facing an overwhelming force, the Spanish surrendered and sued for peace. Rhylia accepted the terms in 1617, with a consequence of ending trade relations with the English and Dutch. Kristiana would pour Rhylia's resources into strengthening the economy, the military, and rebuilding the Norlander territories. She would die in 1652.

Her successor, Christiaan, would take the throne and begin sending massive trade fleets to all European colonies within reach, escorted by Rhylian ships of the line to ensure their safety, declaring the agreement of his mother with Spain to be null and void, and that Rhylia was going to remain neutral in their own way. As such, he began trading unique Rhylian crops and goods with the colonial powers, in exchange for technologies, goods, and literature. Using this new knowledge, he began ordering government patronage of the arts and sciences, promoting a renaissance on Rhylian shores. He would die in 1696.

His successor, Kristiana IV, would take the throne just as the Enlightenment reached Rhylia's shores for the first time. However, unlike in Europe, Rhylia did not embrace humanism, instead viewing it as an insult to their history. With pride in their long tradition of Christian faith and their ancient strength, they instead took the lessons of the Enlightenment as the blueprints to strengthen their existing positions. Kristiana herself became a proponent of new scientific advancements, encouraging Rhylian citizens to question the status quo and seek out new ideas. She abdicated the throne in 1704 due to a belief that monarchy was an obsolete form of governance.

Her successor, Cyana X, did not share this belief, and neither did the Rhylian people, who believed that a Nylani on the throne meant prosperity for Rhylia. She did, however, believe that the people deserved to have a voice, and began implementing an advisory parliament, which could be elected, and itself elect advisors to the crown. The first vote occurred in 1712, after years of deliberation and meticulous design. Sadly, Cyana would die due to health complications in 1714.

Her successor, Kato VI, would take the throne just as foreigners began streaming into Rhylian ports, hailing from Japan, Russia, and the European colonies. Fearing that they may attempt to influence the popular vote into subservience to a foreign crown, he closed the ports. However, his ancestor's plans worked just as she intended them to, and the people's chosen advisors convinced him to reopen the ports and let these newcomers contribute to the growth of Rhylian arts and sciences. Following his decision to do so, the Rhylian economy experienced an unprecedented boom, as universities, companies, and industries began rising on Rhylian shores at unprecedented pace. He would die in 1765, having brought Rhylia to a new age as a regional power.

### The Divide (1765 - 1860 AD)

Kato VI's successor, Kristiana V, took the throne in 1765, to a rapidly changing world. The industrial revolution had already seized Rhylia, and the tides of change were rolling overhead. Additionally, reports of sedition and unease in the Americas challenged the global stability as Rhylia understood it. With the past few centuries having eroded Rhylia's relations with Europe, it was no surprise that Kristiana sided with the American revolutionaries in 1776. Rhylian fleets began to ravage British trade routes in the Pacific, sabotaging their logistics and inciting a declaration of war by their former allies. The Spanish and French capitalized upon this, moving to defend Rhylia's interests in the Pacific as a foil to England. Kristiana unfortunately died in June of 1783, months before the Revolutionary War would end.

Her successor, Mathiel XI, would take the throne, taking part in the peace agreements that ended the Revolutionary War, and establishing Rhylia's first formal embassy of the Industrial Revolution in the American colonies in 1789. He and George Washington would become surprisingly good friends until the latter's death in 1799. Mathiel would himself die in 1808.

His successor, Katarina, would take the throne. Relations with England would sour again when the fledgling US called upon Rhylia as an ally in the War of 1812. Again, Rhylia would attack and upset British interests in the Pacific, until the war's end in 1815. She would also find herself at war with Spain at the same time, in Mexico's war for independence (1810-1821), resulting in a second land invasion of Rhylia in 1818. The sheer stress of fighting two wars simultaneously would shorten her lifespan considerably, causing her to die in 1823 from health complications.

Her successor, Katarina II, would begin developing the relationship between Rhylia and the United States, as well as the one with Mexico. The Mexican towns in Alta California would see Rhylian trade ships come and go every few months, bringing seeds and literature that livened up the local culture. Things were relatively uneventful until 1848, when Rhylian ships brought news of gold being discovered by American settlers in California. Realizing the United States might soon have port access to Rhylia, Katarina began intensifying the trade effort, supplying the settlement at San Francisco with Rhylian goods and food in exchange for newer innovations like denim and American steam locomotives. Using these new designs, Katarina II would order the first Trans-Rhylian railways, as Wild West culture began to seep deep into the Rhylian psyche, especially that of the Norlanders. She would die on February 18, 1860, a mere week after her first train ride.

### Uncertain Times (1860 - 1920 AD)

Katarina II's successor, Adarac, would take the throne in 1860. Overseeing the construction of Rhylia's first ironclads and the rise of a massive steel boom in Rhylia's southeastern, Russian-immigrant dominated [Komiak territory](celesta-public-archive/locations/rhylian-continent/comiryca/index.md), Adarac was convinced he would be seeing a new era of peace for Rhylia. He was caught completely unprepared when his ally in the United States found itself embroiled in a civil war. Believing the cause of the South unjust, he sided with the Union, declaring the use of slaves a grim, unforgivable crime against both God and man. He sent food and early Rhylian rifles to California shores, hoping to provide the Union with war supplies. He would develop a rapport with Lincoln, only to have his hopes shattered once more when Lincoln was assassinated in 1865 after the war's end. He would claim his own life on May 18, 1866 after a long battle with PTSD hallucinations and depression.

His successor, Grayath, would take the throne in 1866 and provide further aid to the United States in the form of monetary grants and supplies. In exchange, former slaves seeking asylum would be welcomed into Rhylia and given the opportunity to have an education and become a part of Rhylian society. This offer was accepted, but American immigrants also began settling in Rhylian territories, especially across the northern shores. Facing this influx of new immigrants, Grayath was pressured to allow dual-citizenship. He ultimately granted it. He would die on June 17, 1901.

His successor, Kalsendyr, would take the throne later that year, investing funds into Rhylia's first electrical grid and telegraph lines, and overseeing sweeping research projects into chemistry, engineering and biology. Hearing rumors of a new mineral taking California by storm, named "celestium," he also began orders to prospect for the mineral on Rhylian soil. Massive deposits would be discovered in the Norlander territories' coastal mountains. In 1904, when construction on the Panama Canal began, he sent financial support and Rhylian workers to assist in its construction. Unlike his predecessors of the 18th and early 19th centuries, he believed it was time to open up trade with Europe and Asia once more, and a canal to cross from the Pacific to the Atlantic would make it worth Rhylia's time to do so. When the canal opened in 1914, he received news of the outbreak of a major war in Europe, and that two sides had formed. He ultimately sided with the Allies, sending them war support and Rhylian literature. He was forced to bring Rhylia into the war on July 13, 1917, when a German submarine sank a Rhylian passenger ship en route to London. Receiving early biplane schematics from the United States, he formed the Rhylian Royal Air Corps. later that year and dedicated over 250,000 troops to the war effort. He would die on February 16, 1920, from heart failure caused by excessive smoking.

### The Rising Red Eagle (1920 - 1968 AD)

Kalsendyr's successor, Haldriel, would take the throne on February 19, 1920. Unfortunate for the young age of 19, she found herself facing a new crisis in Rhylia - the rise of a Marxist-Leninist movement in the Komiak territory. Believing it to have been instigated by the new USSR, she openly condemned Moscow, entering an embargo against the USSR and sending spies to begin an underground railroad for persecuted Jews and Christians on Russian soil. She also kept a wary eye on the movements headed by Hitler and Mussolini in Europe, believing their ideologies to be similarly dangerous. She would open Rhylia's first aluminum and rubber production facilities within the first five years of her rule, and would begin construction of Rhylia's first aircraft carrier and submarines in 1927. Disaster struck when the Great Depression started in October 1929, forcing Haldriel to seize vital industries that would otherwise have failed, keeping them alive at government expense. When the Marxists in Komiak started a revolution on December 13, 1929, Haldriel was forced to grant them independence, remembering a line from ancient prophecy - "the rising red eagle must rustle the nest of the owl, on the eve of the black eagle's first flight." Upon Comiryca's entry into the USSR, Haldriel was forced to mobilize the Rhylian military and protect the new border. Aleria and Rhylia entered a mutual defensive pact under Haldriel's rule, on September 13, 1931. The USSR, via Comiryca, would declare war on Rhylia on February 3, 1932, forcing Rhylia to use its air force and modernized navy for the first time. The casualties inflicted upon Comirycan troops were devastating, resulting in the reclamation of much of Rhylia's southeastern deserts and a peace treaty on January 8, 1933. Haldriel would see the aforementioned line of the prophecy fulfilled upon Hitler's rise to power on January 30 of that same year. Realizing what this meant, she made a public declaration of being the "Iron Queen" of ancient prophecy, and began writing mutual defensive pacts with England, France, the US, and Australia. When World War II officially began in 1939, she mobilized Rhylia's military for war once more, subsidizing new materiel and ordnance factories across the Rhylian heartland, singlehandedly revitalizing the Rhylian economy and producing modern war equipment, including Rhylia's first tank, the 39C Wyvern IA. Upon news that Germany had invaded Sweden and was making plans on France next, she issued a formal declaration of war upon the Axis powers on January 16, 1940. However, Haldriel suspected that she would have to fight a war on the Pacific front, and did not dedicate any substantial troop deployment to Europe. She was proven right in September of 1940, when Japan joined the Axis powers and subsequently declared war on Rhylia. Her response to this was total - she began a massive troop and naval deployment to the Pacific, giving her soldiers orders to show no restraint, knowing full well the Japanese bushido code would mean her own would die if they didn't do anything it took to survive. Her efforts ultimately prevented the Japanese from reaching Australian soil and seriously hindered the Japanese advance on the Philippines. However, without the extensive military infrastructure of the United States, she was unable to maintain an extended front and was forced to start pulling her forces back bit by bit in order to prevent needless casualties. She would manage to hold the line towards the southern parts of Indonesia, but this stalemate would cost many Rhylian lives for nearly a year. Then, in December of 1941, the Japanese struck Pearl Harbor. Seeing news of this, Haldriel was enraged, and began ordering redeployment into Japanese territory. They would pay dearly for attacking non-combatants. When the United States entered the war, Haldriel immediately offered FDR mutual supply lines and cooperative troop deployment. He would accept, and Rhylia would become a crucial part of the Manhattan Project and the Pacific war front. Rhylia began mining uranium on its soil and carefully exporting it to the United States, in exchange for the US being able to establish military bases on Rhylian soil to have a more stable beachhead to launch attacks from. By the end of the war c. 1945-1946, the US had extensive military infrastructure across Rhylia, and even had troops on the Comirycan border. Haldriel would meet with President Truman on January 13, 1947, and personally ask the US to keep its bases and military presence, knowing that America may be the only thing keeping the Soviet Comiryca at bay. Additionally, she would speak with Hirohito and Shigeru Yoshida of Japan, offering a formal apology for the actions taken during wartime, and offering aid to assist with the displaced Japanese civilians and the aftermath of the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. The three countries would ultimately form the Rhyle Pact on July 16, 1947, assuring mutual defense and aid if any of them should be attacked by a nation with presence in the Pacific. Haldriel would be diagnosed with cancer on March 16, 1950, but would ultimately refuse to abdicate the throne, believing that as long as she lived, she would see Rhylia's future secured. She would continue to develop closer ties with the US, even helping in conflicts such as the Korean War and the Vietnam War before her death on September 9, 1968.

### The Waning Era (1968 - 2002 AD)

Haldriel's successor, Myrvaen, would take the throne on August 28, 1968, as his mother's condition finally began to claim her life. Myrvaen himself was already 44 years old by the time he took the throne, and found himself ruling a country that was already waning. Haldriel had taken the cost of the war hard, and had started relying more on the United States for military aid and equipment, resulting in Rhylia's own original equipment becoming dated and obsolete. However, wishing to respect her legacy, he did not pursue the self-remilitarization of Rhylia, believing that the agreement between Rhylia and the US was beneficial for both, with Rhylia already outright paying the US in several ways to maintain its presence. However, knowing that he couldn't leave Rhylia otherwise defenseless, he invested into Rhylian intelligence agencies, forming a global espionage network meant to help keep the USSR and its KGB in check - and likewise, to keep Comiryca as far behind Rhylia as possible. He also tried to strengthen Rhylia's soft power to prevent the US from completely overtaking Rhylia culturally, funding public works projects and national infrastructure that would bring its civilian economy up to par with the US and its allies. By 1982, his efforts had proven fruitful, and the nations of the Rhyle Pact enjoyed a healthy trade and tourism exchange while the USSR began to crumble. However, he had not entirely succeeded in crippling Comiryca as he had hoped. They took the increasing isolation from Moscow and turned it into a weapon, taking their limited technology and advancing upon it in ways that didn't match the rest of the world, taking to mastery of what they had instead of development of "the new thing," and had also invested in espionage programs of their own. It was clear to Myrvaen that his life might soon be in danger, so he looked to the Rhylian Royal Guard Academy for bodyguards, and found a promising young man in the 22 year old Deneferio D'naro, a purebred owl. The young D'naro proved to excel in martial and strategic prowess, impressing Myrvaen and his heir apparent, Alsendyr. Unfortunately, on May 13, 1985, Myrvaen suffered a heart attack. Pressured by his son Alsendyr to abdicate the throne so he could spend his remaining years peacefully, Myrvaen looked upon his options and realized his son was probably right. He abdicated the throne on May 22, 1985, coronating Alsendyr.

Upon taking the throne, Alsendyr immediately appointed D'naro as his military advisor. Following D'naro's advice, he implemented a DMZ across the Comirycan border, and began investing in modernization of the Rhylian military, purchasing M1A1 Abrams tanks and F-15s from the US, and modernizing the navy as well. Additionally, upon the encounters with [Pacifica](celesta-public-archive/locations/pacific-ocean/pacifica/index.md) in 1989, Alsendyr began to establish trade agreements and open borders with the seafloor nation, hoping to leverage them as a foil against the USSR and the rising CCP. Upon the end of the Cold War in 1991, however, Alsendyr realized that the need for fear may well be over. He would begin standing down military developments and working with the US to ease their financial burden of keeping bases in Rhylia, as well as slightly decreasing the patrols along the Comirycan border, despite intelligence and satellite imagery suggesting that Comiryca was hyper-industrializing. This agitated D'naro, who voiced his concerns in 1992. These concerns were ultimately dismissed as a waste of resources and manpower on a target that was deemed a historically-guaranteed failure. As the United States continued to pull out of its defense of Rhylia, Alsendyr pivoted the Rhylian focus towards economic, technological and cultural dominance, a move which further angered D'naro. Over the next 10 years, D'naro would begin to sabotage some of Alsendyr's efforts and stoke dissent and sedition in the Rhylian military, who agreed that the world was leaving Rhylia behind in all the ways that really mattered.

### The Knife (2002 - 2006 AD)

By 2002, the world was changing once more. Alsendyr was growing frustrated with the lack of progress in Rhylia's development and continued to pour more funds into these efforts. D'naro realized that if his concerns were ever to be answered, he would have to start seizing power. So, he began to bribe government officials and gradually consolidate power right underneath Alsendyr's nose, gaining near complete control of Rhylia's intelligence agencies and military forces by January of 2004.

On April 6, 2004, Alsendyr would gain his final child and potential heir, Eledriel Naosano Nylani. Noticing that Alsendyr favored this young child over more 'suitable' heirs, D'naro snapped, believing the Nylani crown was no longer viable, a corrupt and decaying bloodline that was doing more harm to Rhylia than it was good. So, he began cementing his control of the government, drafting plans to coup the crown that ultimately came to fruition on February 15, 2006.

Alsendyr and his family, save for wife Aryka and daughter Eledriel, who escaped, were slaughtered by D'naro's forces in the palace. The Nylani dynasty was over, ended by a knife in the crown's back.

## D'naro Regime - People's Democracy of Rhylia
#### 2006 - 2021 AD
---
### The Scalpel (2006 - 2008 AD)

Sitting on the throne, the palace wrecked from the battle and resulting blaze, Deneferio D'naro issued his first orders. He ordered full military occupation of key Rhylian cities and a crackdown on any dissent, hoping to snuff out loyalists or even finish the Nylani bloodline for good. He also kicked out the remaining US forces and cut all trade and diplomatic ties with them, also pulling Rhylia from the Rhyle pact. In the process, he would wind up harming relations with Aleria, who embargoed his new PDR.

With massive riots and the threat of a civil war, D'naro ordered the bombing of rioters, claiming them as enemy combatants, silencing the media and executing whistleblowers. However, this ultimately backfired. Survivors coordinated and fled to the southwestern deserts, making new settlements and declaring independence as Ravenstead. Not seeing them as a threat, he simply surrounded them with military forces, boxing them in.

By 2007, Rhylia had changed. Once bustling streets were now eerily quiet, soldiers were patrolling in armored cars while citizens kept their heads down and silently went to work. Realizing that Rhylia's military was still inadequate compared to major powers of the era, he began focusing Rhylia's economy almost exclusively on military R&D, even reforming the Royal Guard into his own personal Grey Guard, soldiers equipped with American M4A4s but in heavy armor reminiscent of the dragons of old.

By 2008, his efforts to locate Aryka and Eledriel had failed - however, he assumed that either they were hiding to spend their last days cold and alone, or that they had died in the chaos and were simply unaccounted for. Either way, he had won, and it was time to focus his efforts on taking out Rhylia's great enemy once and for all. He declared war on Comiryca.

### The Sword (2008 - 2015 AD)

A massive strike on Comiryca rocked the world on October 13, 2008. D'naro's forces had carpet bombed several acres of the now completely industrialized Comiryca, leveling concrete and steel into a smoking heap of slag for miles as soldiers rushed in. The resulting battle was a bloodbath, a meat grinder, that would continue on seemingly forever. The global response was as one might expect - the US and UN condemned the attack, China and Russia began supporting Comiryca's war effort, and various countries began taking sides. However, it didn't matter. Comiryca and Rhylia were locked in war and out for the other's blood. D'naro began expanding the Grey Guard, hoping to make every soldier an elite soldier, and Comiryca began R&D into mechs and other massive war machines, hoping to overpower Rhylian forces and prove the glory of the USSR was not yet over.

The war carried on and Rhylia's resources almost entirely became focused on quenching internal rebellions and riots, and fighting Comiryca. Civil infrastructure began to decay and individual towns and villages became increasingly isolated. In 2011, the situation had gotten so bad that Ravenstead declared war, seeing Rhylia as a weak and dying state. However, the severity of conditions in Ravenstead meant that they had become an authoritarian country of their own in order to prevent themselves from starving to death or running dry on other critical resources like water and electricity. Now, with a war machine, this fate was solidified.

Rhylia's condition continued to degrade over the following four years, and D'naro's health began to wane with it.

### The Fire (2015 - 2021 AD)

Partisan movements began to spring up in localized pockets of Rhylia starting in 2015. Small, unorganized, and ultimately ineffective, these movements served as little more than an annoyance on Rhylian logistics and security. Some cells were rounded up and executed, but others managed to be clever enough to avoid being scrutinized, operating with just enough restraint to bleed D'naro's regime of rations and materiel without attracting too much attention, the local officers suspecting involvement but unable to actually prove it. Many of these movements were headed by former Royal Guardsmen who resigned in the wake of the coup, recognizing D'naro as a threat, and still holding to Nylani loyalty.

With the rise of these movements, Rhylia's civil economy almost entirely collapsed. Many were forced to survive on scraps out in the wilderness, building makeshift bunkers in the hills and recycling rusting vehicles into weapons and shelters, forming highwaymen collectives. The war effort in Comiryca was also falling apart with the growing threat of Ravenstead, which was recruiting members of the highwaymen collectives near their borders in order to feed and clothe them, providing them with a new purpose and a new target. D'naro's health continued to wane, the stress of these efforts wearing him down over the next six years.

On February 3, 2021, the now 16-year-old Eledriel Nylani assumed the name "Nyla" and emerged from a cave in Pheasant Point, setting out on a quest to reclaim the throne and restore the Nylani crown as its sole surviving and rightful heir. Over the course of the next few months, she would recruit members and leaders of partisan movements as well as newfound rogues and black marketeers who operated against the D'naro government, crossing the Rhylian heartland and dealing with highwaymen and Grey Guardsmen like an unstoppable force, even entering Rhyle itself for a time to gather intel and sow the seeds of revolution. Having heard enough in Rhyle, she moved on to the Norlander territories, now freshly 17, on a quest to find old royal armor from the temples in the tallest mountains.

On June 8, 2021, Nyla found herself chasing remnants of a prophecy she saw across the temples, seeking out the last location and the artifact within, when an unexpected sight met her, ready for battle - an ice dragon, just barely awoken from slumber. Realizing what this meant, based on the prophecy, she knew she had to win. And win she did, subduing the dragon (named Thrace, or Thrakith in the Old Rhylian tongue) and gaining it as a powerful ally. Upon returning from the mountains a few days later, the Norlanders proclaimed her the "Reclaiming Queen," and pledged fealty to her, one of their young merchants even joining her band. The civil war to reclaim Rhylia was on.

Over the next several months, Nyla would again sweep across the heartland and outer territories of Rhylia, even fighting alongside Rhylian soldiers in Comiryca, earning their loyalty and trust. With the popular backing, she knew it was time to move on Rhyle.

### The Shout (August 21, 2021 - October 17, 2021 AD)

Organizing the people and training them, Nyla formed a militia corps that she tasked with securing and liberating the various towns and villages of Rhylia, building the nation into a civil war that would distract D'naro and render him forced to go on the defensive. Revealing herself openly for the first time, she made a defiant, televised speech on August 21, 2021, that would shock the world - and turn D'naro paranoid. Over the next few months, she would push forward with her band, paving the way towards Rhyle for the front lines to follow.

On October 17, 2021, having finally arrived in Rhyle again, she lit the fuse of uprising in the Rhylian capitol. The Grey Guard were quickly overwhelmed by mobs of rioting civilians and partisans, and even some defectors to Nyla's cause from the Rhylian military turned their rifles against their commanders, the Rhylian version of Bella Ciao being sung in the streets by a populace with renewed hope, vigor and pride. This provided the perfect opportunity for Nyla to breach the capitol and make her way to the throne room, where she would permanently depose Deneferio D'naro, having him arrested.

## Second Nylani Dynasty - Kingdom of Rhylia
#### 2021 - present
---
### The Restoration (November 2, 2021 - 2024 AD)

Finally taking the throne after a solemn coronation, Nyla I took control of the Rhylian military, quelling the last holdouts of the D'naro loyalists and regime with unapologetic force, and calling for truce with Ravenstead, offering them a chance to be brought back into the fold. However, the problem of Comiryca still remained. Realizing that with a weakened country, almost no remaining civil infrastructure, and internal strife that would take months or years to work out, Nyla decided to petition the US for renewed defense and a reinstitution of the Rhyle Pact. Knowing well she may be rejected, the instability of the past two decades being enough to turn even the staunchest Rhylian allies away, she knew she needed to act in the meantime. So, she led the charge against Comiryca herself, like her ancestors of old, sword in hand. With the return of the dragons, she reimplemented the RDC as a part of the Rhylian Air Force, knowing it would revitalize the Rhylian fighting spirit and at least give their weakened infrastructure and military a chance against the Comirycans.

In 2022, Aleria would break the embargo with Rhylia and offer financial and military support in the conflict with Comiryca, joining the war on Rhylia's side, viewing the return of the Nylani crown as evidence enough to return to alliance. Additionally, Ravenstead accepted the terms of truce, but rejected the offer of rejoining Rhylia, citing that they needed hard, long-term proof that the situation in Rhylia would truly improve before they could even consider returning to the motherland. Nyla heard the news while on the front lines, and had to take a moment of solitude, to ponder the situation and pray for guidance and deliverance.

She would get her answer in August of 2023, when the United States would help Rhylia re-establish the Rhyle Pact and join the conflict against Comiryca, noting that CIA reports showed Comiryca had long-term plans against them. Unexpectedly for any of the forces involved, however, a Rhylian nationalist movement sparked a civil war within Comiryca later that year, turning Comirycan streets into a bloodbath of civilian against soldier. The war officially ended on September 4, 2024, with a casualty count far too high for either Comiryca or Rhylia to be comfortable with.

### The Present Situation

In the years since the war with Comiryca ended and the Komiak territory was annexed by Rhylia once more, Ravenstead has reintegrated into Rhylia and Rhylia has entered a new state of peace as a regional power, helping foil the US' enemies with a new economy and a new mutual defense between the two countries. Nyla has re-established the old Nylani government in full, her mother Aryka returning to the palace to act as a familial advisor.

## Demographics

The diversity of Rhylia is difficult to adequately categorize due to the sheer quantity of species. However, three categories can be noted immediately. High Rhylians comprise 11.8% of Rhylia's population, being the core monarchist population and the original founders of City Rhyle; Norlanders comprise 13.4% of the population, having dominated the Northwest of Rhylia and built it into a densely populated region; humans comprise the largest percentage at 22.4% of the population, their ubiquitous nature making them easy to find across the land. The remaining population is a melting pot of other species, with a striking amount of slimes and owls.

With a population of roughly 275 million, Rhylia lacks the manpower to compete globally with major superpowers, but has the population density required to still be a major industrial power and hold their own territory effectively.

## Governance

The Rhylian government is a hybrid constitutional monarchist republic, being ultimately ruled by a monarch family with deep advisory ties to elected officials and governors across the nation, who do have significant regional power. Traditionally, the monarch of Rhylian society has ruled semi-theocratically, but this is not a guarantee nor a rule.

The Rhylian military is composed of the Rhylian Army, the Rhylian Royal Navy, the Rhylian Air Force, the Rhylian Dragon Corps., the Rhylian Royal Guard, and the Rhylian Royal Marines. They are equipped with a mixture of purchased US military equipment and local Rhylian technologies with relative parity to the US military, on a smaller scale.
