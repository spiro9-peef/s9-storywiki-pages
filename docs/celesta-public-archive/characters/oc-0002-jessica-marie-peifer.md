## Biography

### Personal History

Prior to Changewave-1

Jessica Marie Peifer (née Rachels) was born November 10, 2000 to Steven Rachels and Karolina Rachels (née Norris) in Celesta Central Memorial Hospital, along with twin sister Symphony Rachels. Karolina developed stage four breast cancer during the pregnancy and was given an experimental celestium-based radiation treatment which ultimately succeeded in eradicating the cancer, but also exacerbated the Rachels family trait of having high cellular celestium concentrations, resulting in a 2-to-1 ratio of celestium to organic material in the twins and a slightly increased expression of CVCS in both compared to the Rachels family baseline.

These factors made the twins a primary target of international interests. Shortly after birth, several foreign governments attempted to abduct both twins, seeking the unique cellular composition for military and medical research to get a leg up on the United States. These attempts were thwarted but resulted in federal authorities putting security in place around the Rachels household to keep Jessica and Symphony safe, as well as to monitor their health and development, lest the unusually high celestium concentration have unforeseen consequences.

Even with federal oversight and security, the family was not immune to disaster. On October 8, 2005, while being driven home from school, a drunk driver struck the Rachels family vehicle with Jessica and Symphony on board. First responders found Symphony seemingly dead on arrival along with aforementioned drunk driver. Unbeknownst to anyone, however, Symphony had been "flash-stored" as a biological blueprint on Jessica's own bio-integrated celestium deposits, keeping her theoretically alive and cognizant of her surroundings through the lens of Jessica's perception, unable to interact otherwise. With the fact that this was not known, the Rachels family took it as a tragedy and gave Symphony a proper burial and funeral ceremony.

In the following few years, driven by vague memories of her twin sister and the emotional baggage of losing her, alongside her naturally high IQ and an early diagnosis of Asperger's Syndrome, Jessica would quickly breeze through elementary, middle and high school, showing aptitudes for the arts and STEM fields. By 2016 she had established herself as a top student for higher education institutions across the world, and chose CSLA for its emphasis on celestium sciences, seeing this research as a possible in-road to bringing Symphony back, enrolling fall of that year - still unaware that Symphony was a passenger.

In 2017, she would meet [Jeremy Peifer](oc-0001-jeremy-david-peifer.md) on Discord, under his alias at that time (peefTube#9100 in 2017). They quickly made friends due to sharing creative aptitudes, shared interests in technology and gaming, a mutual diagnosis of Asperger's Syndrome, and even some shared loneliness. The two would develop a shared crush on each other that wouldn't go anywhere for nearly two years, due to factors in Jeremy's life that prevented him from pursuing it. In August of 2019 this changed, and the two became much closer friends, quite literally in fact as Jeremy's family moved to Celesta around that time. In September of 2019, the two officially began dating, and on Christmas Day 2019 Jeremy would propose to her, which she ultimately accepted. The two would be married on [March 16, 2020](https://sites.google.com/view/celestapublicarchive-rotcwiki/history/changewave-1?authuser=0).
