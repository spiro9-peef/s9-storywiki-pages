# Cryolasia
#### Genetic Condition

## Synopsis

**Cryolasia, or Retrograde Thermal Sensitivity Spectrum Disorder**, is a genetic condition unique to the ROTC universe. It is caused by [celestium radiation interference](/celesta-public-archive/science/chemistry/celestium/index.md) with an individual's DNA, and can be passed down between generations. It is generally unique to any given individual regardless of if they are biologically related, which means that any two given people with the condition can have dramatically different cases.

As a condition, cryolasia affects the safe external operating temperature range of the body. This manifests as a sensitivity to ambient temperature, as if it were lower than it actually is. The more severe the case, the more dramatic the temperature requirements. As an example, a particularly extreme case of cryolasia can cause an individual to experience hypothermia or even frostbite in normal room temperatures, requiring them to live in Death Valley temperature ranges in order to wear traditional casual clothing. This is offset thanks to smartwear technologies.

Unlike [CVCS](/celesta-public-archive/science/medical/cvcs.md), cryolasia does not have a type identification system. It is too difficult to quantify except as a spectrum disorder.
